# Privacy Policy — Sleep Tracker

**Status:** draft for hosting (GitHub Pages / own domain) before App Store submit  
**Last updated:** 2026-07-31

Sleep Tracker (“the App”) is a personal sleep history tool for iPhone.

## Data the App accesses

### Apple Health (HealthKit)

With your permission, the App may read **sleep analysis** samples from Apple Health (for example data recorded by Apple Watch). That data is processed on your device to show nights, trends, stages, and sleep debt.

We request **read** access only for sleep-related types needed for App Functionality. We do not request access to unrelated Health categories.

### Optional self-hosted server

If you enter a server URL and credentials in Settings, the App may send and receive sleep records **only to that address**, using the authentication you provide. You control that server, its retention, and its security. The developers of Sleep Tracker do not operate a mandatory cloud backend for your nights.

Credentials you enter are stored on your device (app preferences / Keychain as implemented) for connecting to *your* server.

## Data we do not collect

In the default configuration of version 1.x:

- We do **not** require an account.
- We do **not** sell personal data.
- We do **not** include third-party advertising SDKs.
- We do **not** track you across other companies’ apps or websites for advertising.

If a future version adds optional analytics, this policy will be updated before that release and the App Store privacy labels will be updated to match.

## Children’s privacy

The App is not directed at children under 13. Do not use the App to submit personal information of children.

## Medical disclaimer

Sleep Tracker is **not** a medical device and does **not** diagnose, treat, or prevent any disease or condition. Visualizations are for personal insight only. Seek a qualified professional for medical advice.

## Your choices

- Deny or revoke HealthKit access in iOS Settings → Health → Data Access & Devices.
- Leave the optional server fields empty to keep data on-device / HealthKit only.
- Delete the App to remove local configuration on the device.
