Sleep stages

Stage composition per night from wearable data, with approximate adult reference ranges.

Sleep debt

Cumulative shortfall vs your sleep goal. Recent nights weigh more than old ones (debt decays ~10% a day).

What moves your quality

Correlation between your quality ratings and each factor. Correlation is not causation.

Unusual nights

Nights that broke your own pattern (beyond 2.5 standard deviations of your rolling baseline).

Methods: least-squares trend with a t-based 95% confidence interval; circular statistics for clock times; Pearson and Spearman correlation; z-scores against a rolling 30-night baseline; exponentially decaying sleep-debt model.

Stage reference ranges (deep ~13–23%, REM ~20–25% of time asleep) are approximate adult values, and wearable staging is itself an estimate — nothing on this page is medical advice.
